About Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy

Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy is a young aspiring political leader and emerging public voice from Andhra Pradesh. His roots are in Anaparthi, in the East Godavari district, with a connection to Visakhapatnam.

Born on 4 May 2007, Venkat Hruday Reddy has developed an early interest in public life, youth participation, community development and the future of Andhra Pradesh.

His long-term aspiration is to enter politics at a young age and build a public journey centred around service, constructive leadership, opportunity and development.

His vision places particular importance on the role of young people in shaping the future of Andhra Pradesh. Education, employment, technology, healthcare, women's safety, community development, sports and culture are among the areas he wants to focus on.
