About Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy

Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy is a young aspiring political leader from Andhra Pradesh, with roots in Anaparthi in East Godavari district and a connection to Visakhapatnam.

Born on 4 May 2007, he has developed an interest in public life, youth participation and the future of Andhra Pradesh. He aspires to enter politics at a young age and build a journey centered on public service, constructive leadership and opportunities for young people.

His long-term interests include youth development, education, employment, technology, healthcare, community development, women’s safety and social service.
