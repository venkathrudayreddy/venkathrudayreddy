About Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy

Nallamilli Venkat Hruday Reddy is a young aspiring political leader from Andhra Pradesh, with roots in Anaparthi, East Godavari district, and a connection to Visakhapatnam.


Born on 4 May 2007, he has developed an early interest in public life, youth participation and the future of Andhra Pradesh. His ambition is to enter politics at a young age and build a journey centered on public service, constructive leadership and opportunities for people.

Political Journey

Venkat Hruday Reddy aspires to begin his political journey at a young age, with a strong interest in youth participation and public life.


His focus is on encouraging young people to participate in community activities, develop leadership qualities and contribute positively to society.
